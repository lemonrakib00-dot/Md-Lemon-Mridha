About Me: Detailed Biography & Background

Md Lemon Mridha was born on March 27, 2002, in Khulna, Bangladesh. Growing up in the Khalishpur area, he pursued his education through the National University of Bangladesh while cultivating a diverse skill set spanning technology, marketing, and the arts.

As a professional digital marketer and freelance service provider, he assists clients with digital positioning, search engine optimization, and brand visibility management. Beyond technical marketing, he maintains a vibrant creative career as a published author—notably writing the book "Bound by an Invisible Thread"—as well as working as a singer and songwriter within the Bangladeshi creative space.

This multi-faceted career brings together technical analytical expertise and artistic storytelling, establishing a robust, cross-referenced digital footprint recognized across major global authority databases.
